About

What is a QR code?

QR code, which stands for Quick Response code, is a two-dimensional barcode which can be easily read by a machine, such as a mobile phone. A typical QR code exists of a pattern made out of black and white squares. The pattern can be decoded to extract the data it contains. The QR code is invented by Denso Wave to mark individual components inside their logistics, and create a more efficient process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the QR codes free to use?

Yes, all the QR codes generated on this website are free to use for any purpose, including commercial use.

How long are the QR codes valid for?

The QR codes are valid forever. They are static and the content can't be altered afterwards, but you will be able to scan them forever.

The QR code does not work/scan

Check that the content is correct and ensure there is enough contrast between foreground and background colors. Try increasing the QR code size or decreasing the content size.

Which formats are supported?

We support vector files such as SVG, and image formats including PNG, JPEG, and WEBP. We also support PDF format for high-quality print output.
